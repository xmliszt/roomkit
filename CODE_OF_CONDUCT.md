# Code of conduct

Treat other participants with respect. Welcome people with different backgrounds and levels of experience. Keep feedback specific to the contribution and give people room to disagree.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, unwanted sexual attention, and sharing someone else’s private information without permission are unacceptable. This applies in Roomkit issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project spaces.

Use GitHub’s private reporting tools for abuse. If you can safely contact a maintainer in private, you can also share links and context with them. Maintainers may remove content or limit participation to protect the community.
